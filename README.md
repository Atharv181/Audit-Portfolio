## About Me

I'm a smart contract security researcher who helps DeFi teams find critical bugs before mainnet. I've been auditing competitively since January 2024, and I've also served as a **Lead Judge** for Sherlock audit contests.

I work in **Solidity**, **Rust**, **Go** and **Clarity**, and I write PoCs with **Foundry**. Most of my work is on DeFi protocols: lending markets, LSTs and restaking, stablecoins and synthetic dollars, AMMs, launchpads and NFT infrastructure.

Before security, I was a blockchain developer at BlockTheory, working on the Manta Network explorer (Go), account abstraction, and Arbitrum Orbit / OP Stack projects. Building taught me how protocols are put together; auditing taught me how they fall apart.

I'm currently a fellow in **yAudit's yBorg** program, working on AI-assisted vulnerability discovery.
